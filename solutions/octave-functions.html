A Few GNU Octave Function Files

2026-11-20

Part of a Series on Solutions to Problems

I learned to use Matlab as part of my education, but its proprietary licensing and sluggish GUI application quickly drove me to use GNU Octave instead. I typically use it for most routine numerical programming applications and very often as a general scripting language. Some might scoff at that, but I don't enjoy writing Python.

In my work, I've developed various functions, some of which are more generally useful. Here are a few:

  • consecMedian.m: Takes the median of consecutive runs of a function.
  • savedat.m: Save a tab-separated variable file with headers.
  • systemsolver.m
These are of interest mostly only to electrical engineers:
  • db.m
  • par.m Takes the parallel combination of its inputs.
  • s2pImport.m
  • s2pExport.m
  • s2t.m
  • Srenorm.m
